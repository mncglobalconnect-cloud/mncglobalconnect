Based on NASA imagery and elevation data, as described by Solar System Scope.

Modifications for MNC Global Connect: resized and converted to WebP
(day-8k, day-4k, day-mobile, night, night-mobile, clouds); specular map
inverted/rescaled for roughness; static fallback generated by orthographic
projection and lighting of the day texture.
